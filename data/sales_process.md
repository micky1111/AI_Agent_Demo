# Sales Process — Sunset Meadows Residences

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DATA — for demo purposes only.**

A fictional end-to-end purchase flow for a prospective buyer.

## Step 1 — Browse Availability

Review the [Price List](pricing.md) and [Floor Plans](floor_plans.md)
to shortlist candidate units by type, floor, and budget.

## Step 2 — Reserve a Unit

Pay the fictional reservation deposit described in
[Payment Terms](payment_terms.md). This holds the unit at the quoted
price for 7 days, per [Company Policies](policies.md).

## Step 3 — Sign the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA)

Within the hold window, the buyer and Meadowbrook Developments (fictional
entity) execute the SPA, locking in the unit, price, and payment
schedule.

## Step 4 — Select Finishes

If reserved before structural completion, the buyer selects a finish
package (Essential, Comfort, or Premium — see [FAQ](faq.md)).

## Step 5 — Milestone Payments

The buyer pays each construction milestone per
[Payment Terms](payment_terms.md) as the fictional build progresses.

## Step 6 — Pre-Handover Inspection

A joint walkthrough is scheduled once practical completion is reached,
documenting any snag-list items for resolution before handover.

## Step 7 — Title Transfer & Handover

Final balance is paid, title is registered in the buyer's name (fictional
registry), and keys/access fobs are issued.

## Step 8 — After-Sales Support

A fictional 12-month defects liability period applies post-handover for
workmanship issues — see [Legal Disclaimer](legal_disclaimer.md) for
general terms.
