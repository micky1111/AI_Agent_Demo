# Payment Terms — Sunset Meadows Residences

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DATA — for demo purposes only.**

Fictional milestone-based payment schedule, expressed as a percentage of
the final unit price shown in the [Price List](pricing.md).

| Milestone                                   | % of Price | Notes (fictional)                         |
|----------------------------------------------|-----------:|---------------------------------------------|
| Reservation deposit                           | 2%         | Refundable within the 7-day hold window.    |
| Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) signing       | 8%         | Due within 14 days of reservation.          |
| Foundation complete                           | 15%        | Milestone verified by site inspection.      |
| Structural frame complete (floor 10 topped)   | 20%        |                                              |
| Building envelope / facade complete           | 20%        |                                              |
| Interior finishes complete                    | 20%        |                                              |
| Practical completion / handover               | 10%        | Includes final inspection walkthrough.      |
| Title transfer & registration                 | 5%         | Final balance, due at closing.              |

## Late Payment

- Payments more than 14 days overdue accrue a fictional 1.5% monthly
  late fee on the outstanding milestone amount.
- Payments more than 60 days overdue may result in cancellation per
  [Company Policies](policies.md).

## Early Settlement

- Buyers may pay remaining milestones early in full at any time with no
  prepayment penalty in this fictional scenario.

## Currency & Taxes

- All figures are in fictional currency units and exclude VAT/transfer
  taxes (see [Price List](pricing.md) notes).

## Financing

- This fictional demo does not model third-party mortgage financing;
  all figures assume direct buyer-to-developer payment.
