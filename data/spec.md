# Technical Specifications — Sunset Meadows Residences

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DATA — for demo purposes only.**

## Structure

- **Building type:** Reinforced concrete frame, 10 above-ground floors + 2
  basement parking levels.
- **Seismic design:** Rated for Zone 3 equivalent loading (fictional
  standard "SM-Seismic-R3").
- **Foundation:** Piled raft foundation, engineered for local soil
  conditions (fictional soil report ref. GS-2025-114).

## Envelope & Finishes

- **Exterior cladding:** Fiber-cement rainscreen panels with mineral wool
  insulation (thermal transmittance U ≈ 0.28 W/m²K, fictional spec).
- **Windows:** Double-glazed, low-E coated, aluminum frame, acoustic
  rating Rw 32 dB.
- **Interior flooring:** Engineered oak in living areas, porcelain tile in
  wet rooms.
- **Interior walls:** Gypsum board partitions on metal studs, acoustic
  batt insulation between units.

## Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing (MEP)

- **HVAC:** Individual-unit variable refrigerant flow (VRF) heat
  pump systems, zoned per room.
- **Electrical:** 3-phase supply to building; each unit metered
  individually; smart-home wiring pre-installed (lighting + shades).
- **Plumbing:** Centralized hot water via rooftop solar-assisted boiler
  plant; low-flow fixtures throughout.
- **Fire safety:** Sprinkler system in all common areas and units,
  addressable smoke detection, two independent egress stairwells.

## Energy & Sustainability

- **Energy rating:** Fictional "Class B" rating under the fictional
  national energy code.
- **Renewables:** Rooftop solar array offsets common-area electrical load.
- **Water:** Greywater recycling for landscape irrigation.

## Elevators & Accessibility

- Two passenger elevators (fictional capacity 800 kg / 10 persons each),
  one dedicated service elevator.
- Step-free access from the main lobby to all units and amenity floors.

## Parking & Storage

- Two basement levels with EV-charging-ready bays.
- Private storage cages available for an additional fictional fee — see
  [Price List](pricing.md).
