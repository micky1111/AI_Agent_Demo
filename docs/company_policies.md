# Company Policies — Meadowbrook Developments (Fictional)

> **SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL DATA — for demo purposes only.** "Meadowbrook
> Developments" is an invented company created solely for this demo.

## Reservation Policy

- A unit may be held for 7 calendar days with a refundable reservation
  deposit of a fictional 5,000 currency units.
- Reservations are first-come, first-served and are not transferable
  between buyers without written approval.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy

- Reservation deposits are **fully refundable** if canceled within the
  7-day hold window.
- After a Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) is signed, cancellations follow
  the schedule in [Payment Terms](payment_terms.md); deposits beyond the
  initial reservation amount become partially non-refundable per the
  milestone reached.

## Price Change Policy

- Prices published in the [Price List](price_list.md) may change without
  notice for unsold, unreserved units.
- Once a reservation deposit is accepted, the quoted price is locked for
  that unit for the duration of the hold period and subsequent SPA
  process.

## Customer Data Policy

- Buyer personal data collected during the [Sales Process](sales_process.md)
  is used solely for transaction processing and regulatory compliance
  (fictional "Meadowbrook Data Charter").
- Data is retained for a fictional 7 years post-completion for legal
  record-keeping, then securely destroyed.

## Code of Conduct (Sales Staff)

- Sales representatives must disclose all mandatory fees and taxes before
  a reservation deposit is accepted.
- No sales representative may guarantee future resale value or investment
  returns.
- All marketing materials must carry the synthetic-demo disclaimer where
  applicable in this repository.

## Complaints & Dispute Resolution

- Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days and resolved or
  escalated within 14 business days.
- Unresolved disputes proceed to the fictional "Meadowbrook Independent
  Mediation Panel" before any litigation step.
